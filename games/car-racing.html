HOW TO PLAY

Pretend you're holding a steering wheel — the game will read your hands!
1
🖐️

Hold the wheel

Stand in front of the camera and put both hands in front of your chest like you're gripping a steering wheel.

2
🔄

Turn to steer

Tilt your hands left or right — your car follows the angle of the imaginary wheel.

3
🪙

Eat the coins

Drive into gold coins to score points. Chain them for combo multipliers (x2, x5, x10!).

4
🚧

Dodge the cones

Hit a cone and you lose a heart + your combo resets. Lose all 5 hearts and your race ends.

💡 Tip: 2 players? Player 1 stands on the LEFT half of the camera, Player 2 on the RIGHT. Each one has their own lane!
