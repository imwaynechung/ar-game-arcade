HOW TO PLAY

⚡ STAND IN FRONT OF THE CAMERA · GRAB A FRIEND · ROW! ⚡
1
👫

Pick Your Side

Stand side-by-side in front of the camera. LEFT player rows for 🔴 TEAM RED, RIGHT player rows for 🔵 TEAM BLUE.

2
🤲

Grab the Paddle

Bring both hands together in front of you. A glowing paddle will appear on screen — you're ready to row!

3
🚣

Row Row Row!

Lift the paddle above your shoulders, then dip it down past your hips. That's one stroke! Repeat fast!

4
🔥

Build Combos

Fast back-to-back strokes build a COMBO multiplier up to x20. The faster you row, the further your boat flies!

⏱️ 60 seconds — first boat to cross the finish line wins. If time runs out, highest score takes the trophy! 🏆
